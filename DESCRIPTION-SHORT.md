# CobbleTracker

A free Cobblemon companion mod that catches the rare spawns you'd otherwise walk past — and shows you where they are.

**Spawn beam** — a beacon-style column of light stands over a tracked Pokémon while it's in your loaded chunks. Coloured by tier, comes back if you leave and return, and disappears the moment it's caught, defeated or despawns.

**Minimap waypoints** — announcements carry a clickable *Create Waypoint* link for Xaero's Minimap, VoxelMap and JourneyMap. Only the formats your client actually has get sent, and no minimap mod is required — without one you still get the beam.

**Announcements** — rare spawns are announced in chat, with an optional on-screen title and a sound. Server-wide, or only to players nearby.

**Spawn history** — a GUI listing everything that has spawned, with sprite cards, a tab per category, and sorting by newest, distance, species, tier or who caught it. Survives restarts. There's a plain-chat version that works even without the client mod installed.

**Top Hunters** — a leaderboard ranking players by captures.

**Hunt mode** — press `\` to search the species list and pick what you're hunting. Matching Pokémon near you get their own beam plus directional arrows on your HUD. Entirely client-side.

**Your own categories** — Legends, Shinies, Bosses and Ultra Beasts out of the box. Add, edit or remove categories freely; each brings its own colour, announcement, history and matching rules.

**Optional spawner** — any category can spawn its tier on a schedule, spread fairly across online players so rates don't balloon on a busy server. Off by default.

**Themes and an admin panel** — six GUI themes, and an in-game panel where operators change settings live without touching files.

---

Minecraft **1.21.1** · **Fabric** and **NeoForge** · Cobblemon **1.7.0**

Requires Cobblemon, Architectury API, and Fabric API (+ Fabric Language Kotlin) or NeoForge.
Install on the server for announcements and history; add it on the client for the beam, the GUI, waypoints and hunt mode.

Completely free.
